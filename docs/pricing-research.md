## Method and proposed starting prices

Use the **median** of the closest relevant published offerings, multiplied by **0.80**. A median represents a typical observation better than allowing a single luxury outlier to dominate a small sample. These are not identical packages: minutes, edited-image counts, photographer level and inclusions vary. Therefore the result is a transparent positioning proposal, not a statistically representative market rate or a promise to beat every competitor by 20%.

| Portraits / missionaries | Megan 350; Karisa 275; LaBuervenich 220 | $275 | $220 | $220 |
| Seniors / graduates | Megan 350; Karisa 225; Kylee/Regie 395 | $350 | $280 | $280 |
| Couples | Emily 600; Megan 350; Karisa 325; Justin 950 | $475 | $380 | $380 |
| Proposals | Emily 550; Karisa 325 | $437.50 | $350 | $350 |

The couples figure is particularly provisional because Justin's page carries an updating notice. Excluding that observation would make the three-provider median $350 and the discounted figure $280. Confirm the current quote before treating $380 as a firm benchmark. Proposal coverage is a two-provider sample. Different numbers of observations are intentional: missing public prices should not be invented to fill a table.

The final family and senior proposals use the more specific full-session/associate pages, rather than an ambiguous general “starting at” figure. This explains revisions from any preliminary figures discussed during research.

Events, extended families and commercial work remain quote-based. Wedding research is recorded for context, but the site does not claim a wedding specialty or fabricate wedding galleries from proposal images.

## Package terms are proposed

Session lengths, image counts, group limits and inclusions written into the draft site are proposed offers for Trevor to approve. They are not inferred facts about his existing contracts. Review editing time, travel, delivery capacity, expenses and desired earnings before accepting bookings. No automatic discount badge or competitor comparison appears on customer-facing pages.
