# Launch and search growth

## Content decisions before launch

1. Confirm proposed package prices, minutes, edited-image counts, family group limits and included travel. Delivery timing, deposit, cancellation, weather, rescheduling and image-use terms should match your actual client agreement.
2. Replace or approve Xandra's placeholder. Set `testimonialApproved` true only when the quotation and permission to display it are real. Production builds omit it otherwise; no review schema or invented star ratings are present.
3. Add a real headshot when available. The current about page deliberately uses your photographic work and text, without presenting a client as you.
4. Verify the social links lead to the intended business accounts and check the email/telephone from your own phone.
5. Choose whether the honest email-draft inquiry flow is enough for launch. If you want submissions delivered without opening an email app, connect a form provider and test delivery, errors, spam handling and its privacy disclosures.
6. Pick the GitHub repository and deploy. Connect the domain when purchased. Enable `PUBLISH_READY=true` only after the draft offers are approved. A noindex review site is not private.

## Already implemented

- Crawlable static HTML for every page rather than client-only routing.
- Distinct page titles and descriptions, one H1 per page, descriptive navigation and dedicated service pages.
- Natural Provo, Orem and Utah County context; no near-duplicate doorway pages or keyword stuffing.
- Responsive WebP photographs with descriptive filenames, alt text, explicit dimensions and lazy loading below the main image.
- Self-hosted licensed fonts, small JavaScript, no tracking scripts or large runtime dependencies.
- ProfessionalService structured data with actual provided contact details and service areas; no invented address, credentials, awards or ratings.
- Production canonical URLs, social-sharing metadata, sitemap, robots file and custom 404.
- Keyboard-friendly controls, native dialog lightbox, focus handling, visible focus indicators, reduced-motion support and readable color relationships.

## First 30 days after launch

- Verify the domain in Google Search Console; submit the production sitemap and inspect the main service pages for indexing issues.
- Create or improve the legitimate Google Business Profile, if eligible, using the actual service area and contact details. Do not invent an office or display a home address as a studio.
- Ask real clients for honest reviews and link the profile from the site when verified.
- Replace general portfolio samples over time with complete, permission-cleared session stories. Include useful location context only when accurate.
- Test the deployed site on your own iPhone/Android and a desktop. Run PageSpeed Insights on the real public URL; do not interpret a local test as measured real-user Core Web Vitals.

## Next 60–90 days

- Publish useful, first-hand location/session guides supported by your own photographs and experience.
- Expand the family, senior and proposal galleries with whole sessions, not repeated photos across many thin pages.
- Seek legitimate local references from venues, collaborators and community organizations you actually work with.
- Review Search Console impressions, queries, clicks and actual inquiries monthly. Improve pages based on the questions customers ask.

First-page Google visibility cannot be promised. The build establishes technical foundations; local relevance, reputation, competition and continued useful content determine results over time.

## Design rationale

The references informed principles: AV Potter's breathing room and photographic rhythm, Branson Maxwell's restrained typography and image emphasis, and LaBuervenich's immersive storytelling. This is an original composition, not copied layouts, wording, branding or photographs.

The palette is defined in OKLCH with sRGB fallbacks. Paper and sand share a warm hue family (85–86°); ink, olive and muted text share a botanical family (120–127°); brass (78°) relates to the paper while adding modest chroma. Large shifts in lightness create text contrast without saturated UI competing with the photographs. Cormorant Garamond supplies expressive headings, while Manrope carries navigation and readable practical details.
